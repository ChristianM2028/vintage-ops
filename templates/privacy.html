Privacy

We use order contact, delivery, payment-status, and support information to fulfil purchases, prevent fraud, meet accounting obligations, and resolve disputes. Payment card details are handled by Stripe and are not stored here.

Customer contact, address, notes, and tracking details are retained for up to two years, then anonymized; non-identifying transaction totals and provider references may be retained for accounting. Encrypted backups expire under the backup retention schedule rather than being edited.

To request access, correction, a portable copy, or deletion, contact the store using your order reference. We verify control of the order contact channel before acting and respond within 30 days, subject to legal retention duties.
